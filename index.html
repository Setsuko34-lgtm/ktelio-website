The model

One clear choice, no confusion

Every route gives you two honest options. No account, no hidden fees, no guesswork.

Book it yourself — free

Where we've mapped it, we link you straight to the official regional KTEL page. Elsewhere, we point you to a search that gets you there. No fee, no middleman. Just clarity.

Let us handle it — €5

Local sites, Greek-only forms, phone-only ticket offices — we deal with it and send your ticket straight to you.

Fully transparent

We're an independent booking helper, not affiliated with any KTEL operator. We say so everywhere, on purpose.

Transparency

Why the free link exists

  • We show you the free, official option first, every time — even though it earns us nothing.
  • We only ask for the personal details a booking actually requires, and never keep them longer than needed.
  • We're upfront on every forum and post: independent service, not an official KTEL page.
  • You only pay our €5 fee — your ticket cost is always settled separately and clearly.
  • We track which option you choose, free or paid — never who you are — just to learn honestly whether this service is worth building further.
A note on who we are.

Ktelio is an independent booking assistant. We are not affiliated with KTEL or any regional bus operator. We simply make it easier for travelers to find and book routes that are otherwise scattered across dozens of separate local systems, most of them Greek-only.

Have a question before booking? Use the chat bubble in the corner — a real person replies, not a bot.
